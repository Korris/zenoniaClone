2. Skill-by-skill critique
01. Power Slash — 5.5/10

The close-up sequence shows better hit sparks and increased intensity. But the 650 ms finisher becomes an orange-red circular explosion encompassing the enemies, rather than a massive asymmetric sword slash.

Fix: Remove the filled circular finisher. Use a forward-facing crescent 1.7–2.0H across, with ribbon width 0.22–0.28H, a leading-edge white strip only 0.035–0.055H thick, and a 0.45–0.65H impact star at the target. Keep the final shockwave subtle, alpha ≤0.45.

02. Whirlwind — 5.7/10

The circular attack is more readable on both maps, and the stronger orange edge is an improvement. But the bright ring dominates so completely that the Warrior appears to stand inside a glowing disc.

Fix: Introduce two independently rotating thick crescent meshes, each covering 120–155° of the circumference. Their red-orange bodies should be approximately 0.18–0.22H thick. Reduce the continuous ring's brightness by 45–55%. Retain a faint outer wind shockwave.

03. Flame Cleave — 5.2/10

This is one of the largest remaining failures. In the close-ups:

At 120–220 ms, the effect grows into a filled orange sector.

At 220 ms, a sawtooth-like perimeter dominates the silhouette.

At 400 ms, pale yellow flame pillars cover the target.

By 650 ms, the main crescent is gone and only small residue remains.

The final result feels more like a circular fire spell than a heavy directional sword attack.

Fix: Reserve 65–75% of the visual weight for the forward-moving flaming crescent. Reduce the flash area by 65–75%; keep only 3–5 secondary pillars, each 0.5–0.9H tall. Red outer flames must remain visible around yellow cores.

04. Charging Strike — 5.3/10

The hit at the later sampled moment is brighter and has an identifiable impact. However, the large circular glow is not shaped like a high-speed collision, and the projectile/dash trajectory is not visually continuous enough.

Fix: Replace the circular burst with a forward-pointing shock cone: 2.0H long, 0.9–1.2H wide. Add an elongated triangular white impact core, 0.4–0.6H across, and a narrowing red trail behind the Warrior. Restrict the circular shockwave to ≤0.35 opacity.

05. Earth Break — 6.3/10

Best-performing skill in Round 1. The descending red-gold sword is much more prominent than before, the gold energy is visible against snow, and the final frame conveys a substantial AoE.

But the close-up exposes major issues:

The sword still resembles a long, flat, luminous icon rather than a massive weapon.

The circle under the sword is too uniform.

The later impact becomes an enormous white-orange disc with a glowing ring.

Cracks and debris are underrepresented compared with the blast.

The extended glowing area competes with the hero and monsters.

Fix: Increase blade width by approximately 60–80%, darken its red sidewalls, and replace the filled blast with an annular shockwave surrounding a smaller white-hot center. Make 6–8 cracks and the debris burst distinctly visible during the post-impact hold.

06. Blade Cyclone — 5.2/10

The issue is fundamental: visually, it still reads like Whirlwind with a larger orange ring. The two abilities remain difficult to distinguish at gameplay zoom.

Fix: Eliminate the large filled ground circle. Make the dominant shape a tall helical structure using three ribbon strips, with a projected height of 1.6–1.9H and ground diameter of 2.4–2.8H. The ribbons should pass alternately in front of and behind the character while leaving the face visible.

07. Inferno Crash — 6.0/10

The impact has more intensity and energy. But the dominant shape remains a flat orange disc with multiple yellow pillars.

Compared with the concept image, it lacks a convincing central crater, a strong dark inner silhouette, and irregular black debris. The result looks like an upgraded ring explosion rather than a meteor-impact ultimate.

Fix: Use a 0.5–0.8H central white-hot collision area, a 2.0–2.5H tall fire pillar, a 4.5–5.0H annular shockwave, and a distinctly dark fractured crater occupying approximately 2.3–2.8H in diameter.

08. Berserker Awakening — 5.4/10

The vertical red beam is a major improvement. The skill is easier to identify, especially in the cave. But the transformation remains visually ordinary because the beam, floor circle, and character aura are not coordinated into a convincing burst of power.

Fix: Add a concentrated 0.8–1.0H tall red-white surge around the Warrior's upper body, a 2.2–2.6H dark-red pressure wave, and 3–4 rising flame ribbons. After activation, reduce the aura to 15–22% opacity and keep it within 0.15–0.25H of the character silhouette.

Important shader correction: fix hollow shockwaves first

I suspect the ring shader is applying opacity to the entire quad or its radial gradient is inverted/overly broad. The screenshots cannot prove the cause, but the visual output is unmistakably a filled disc.

A proper ring needs independent inner and outer alpha boundaries, not just a bright circular texture.

Use a normalized radial coordinate 
𝑟
r from 0 at the center to 1 at the outside:

hlsl
float inner = smoothstep(0.80, 0.86, r);
float outer = 1.0 - smoothstep(0.94, 1.00, r);

float ringAlpha = inner * outer * _Opacity;

At the center, alpha must be exactly zero. Use premultiplied-alpha compositing where appropriate, and make sure emissive RGB is also masked by ringAlpha; otherwise transparent portions may still bloom in some material setups.

For an expanding ring, animate its mesh radius. Don't fill the interior with an expanding opaque quad.

Final decision

Round 1: 5.6/10 — not approved.

I would freeze particle-count increases and overall bloom adjustments until the P0 fixes are complete. More particles will only make the existing discs more overwhelming.

The rebuild has established a usable foundation: reds are stronger, impacts are visible, and dark-map contrast is adequate. Now it needs the part that matters most: sculpted shapes, clean alpha, directional forces, and disciplined impact layering.

If Round 2 fixes the opaque discs, restores Flame Cleave's dominant crescent, and distinctly separates Whirlwind from Blade Cyclone, I would expect the overall quality to move toward 7.0–7.5/10, with Earth Break and Inferno Crash potentially higher.

The path to 9/10 is not adding more glow. It's making every attack instantly recognizable from its silhouette alone.
