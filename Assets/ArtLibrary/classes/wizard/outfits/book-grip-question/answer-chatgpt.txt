1. Exactly what to change
Body art (T0-T9)
- Keep the right staff hand unchanged.
- Repaint the left forearm slightly forward, elbow bent, with the palm facing upward.
- Replace the closed fist with a shallow, cupped palm.
- Make the forearm visibly connect to the underside of the book.
- Use the same basic hand pose across tiers; adjust the arm position slightly for larger books.
- Important: the hand should be beneath the book's inner/lower corner, not centred on its decorative front cover.

Book art and pivot
- Keep the full book silhouette and approved tier-specific artwork.
- Set the pivot at the bottom inside edge near the spine, where the palm supports its weight.
- For T7-T9, move the contact point slightly outward if necessary to balance the wider book.
- Don't use the book's geometric centre as the attachment point.
- Give each directional book sprite its own pivot/contact offset.
- At 85 px hero height the books are ~21-35 px; even a 2-3 px grip misalignment becomes obvious.

Layer order - facing down (back to front)
1 Body + forearm + open palm
2 Complete book overlay
3 Small thumb/fingertip overlay (only 2-4 meaningful pixels at game resolution: thumb crossing the lower rim, maybe one fingertip; don't paste the whole fist over the cover)
4 Optional magic glow above pages
For back/side directions change sorting and hand visibility as appropriate; don't force the front-facing arrangement onto every direction.

2. Four-frame breathing idle
The book follows the supporting hand exactly, no independent bobbing. Anchor on the palm's support point every frame.
Frame 1 neutral (0,0); 2 slight inhale (0,-1 px); 3 max inhale (0,-1 px); 4 exhale (0,0) - examples, adjust to the actual painted hand positions.
Rules: book, palm and thumb move together (relative attachment never changes); if breathing shifts the arm sideways, reproduce it in the anchor; integer-pixel offsets at render resolution, point filtering, no subpixel jitter; store the actual hand position per frame.

3. Walking and attacking in four directions
Down: open palm underneath, thumb over lower edge. Up: book seen from behind, hand partly hidden, supported from below. Left: book near torso, palm underneath, side-view book art. Right: book projects forward slightly, forearm visible beneath.
Walk: keep the book on the hand anchor; reduce the left arm swing so the book looks stable.
Attack: casting pose (hand raises book slightly, pages up/outward) + recovery pose (back to rest).
A front-view book cannot just be rotated into a convincing back/side view: needs directional variants.

4. Should T9 float instead?
Keep T0-T9 physically supported, with increasingly elaborate magic effects; a subtle rune circle above T7-T9 is fine. Avoid a large glow between book and palm (looks detached again).

Practical refinement: implement on T9 first (worst-case size); test 4 idle frames, 4 walk directions and one cast at actual 85 px; if T9 reads as "palm supporting book, thumb securing edge", the smaller tiers are straightforward.
